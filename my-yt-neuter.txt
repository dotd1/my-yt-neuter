! [Y32] Rolling numbers + live metadata updates.
!   What it does: blocks the two scripts that animate the view / like counters as they roll,
!   and the request YouTube repeats while you watch to refresh title, views and likes.
!   Effect: counters show a fixed number; the live viewer count on streams stops updating.
!   Risk: scheduler.js is a general YouTube helper. If a future YouTube update moves other work
!   into it, something can stop working without warning. If anything on the watch page acts
!   strange, disable the scheduler.js line first.
||www.youtube.com/s/desktop/*/jsbin/web-animations-next-lite.min.vflset/web-animations-next-lite.min.js$script,domain=youtube.com
||www.youtube.com/s/desktop/*/jsbin/scheduler.vflset/scheduler.js$script,domain=youtube.com
||www.youtube.com/youtubei/v1/updated_metadata$domain=youtube.com
